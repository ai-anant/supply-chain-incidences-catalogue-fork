Poisoned Pipeline / Incidents / AS331

AS331 · 2026-10

OpenCode Console breach hit users who had linked external AI providers

AI coding agents

OpenCode, the open-source coding agent, disclosed a security breach
of OpenCode Console on 17 October 2026. The vendor's post, at 18:08
UTC, says the breach the night before was a large-scale distillation
attack and that it affected 1.4 percent of Console users: the subset
who had external AI providers linked. OpenCode named Anthropic,
OpenAI, and Google as the providers it was working with to remediate.
It said every impacted user was emailed that morning, and that a user
who did not get the email was not impacted. The incident was still
ongoing, and the post gave no root cause and did not say what data
was accessed.

StepSecurity's 18 October write-up quotes that statement and treats
the linked provider credentials as the thing at risk. That inference
is the lab's, not a vendor confirmation that tokens were copied.
The vendor scoped the breach to Console accounts with linked
providers, not to every install of the OpenCode CLI, and published
no affected version list. How the Console was entered is unpublished.

T0180 · Initial Access · Compromise services / servers

OpenCode said its Console was breached and that the affected
accounts were those with external providers linked. The entry
vector is unpublished. Not mapped as a malicious package or
as a compromised developer workstation.

Framework gaps noted

  • Whether linked Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google credentials were copied is unpublished. The vendor said it was working with those providers to remediate, and that the incident was ongoing.
  • No root cause, no affected CLI versions, and no statement of what data was accessed. A local auth.json path in a response guide is not evidence the workstation was the breach.
  • Same week as other disclosures aimed at AI-provider credentials. OpenCode does not name those other companies. Not merged.
