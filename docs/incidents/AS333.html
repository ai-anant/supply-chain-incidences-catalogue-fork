Poisoned Pipeline / Incidents / AS333

AS333 · 2026-10

An attacker accessed Zeabur internal systems and some customer services on 14 October

CI/CD SaaS

Zeabur's founder, Yuanlin Lin, wrote on 17 October 2026 that an
external attacker gained unauthorized access on 14 October (UTC) to
Zeabur's internal systems and to some customer services. The post
says this incident affected a broader scope than an incident in late
August. Zeabur said it had contained known attack paths and was still
investigating. It did not name the actor, the entry vector, or what
was read or changed on customer services. It is accepting compensation
claims from customers who suffered losses because of the incident.

The same post announces that Zeabur is winding down. Dedicated
servers and shared clusters stop by 31 December 2026. That shutdown
is a business decision the founder ties to two security incidents
plus architecture and market limits. It is not itself the attack.
The August incident is a separate, earlier event. This story covers
only the 14 October access the October post describes.

Framework gaps noted

  • Entry vector, actor, and the data taken from customer services are unpublished. Not mapped as a stolen token, a named vulnerability, or a poisoned deployment artifact.
  • The late August incident is a prior event. This post does not give it a primary URL, so it is not a second story.
  • Not merged with other October platform disclosures. Zeabur does not describe AI-provider key theft or a distillation objective.
